# RCA — RCA-2026-14 UPI Lite PSP callback latency increase

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-14  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-14  ·  **Author:** Yash Gupta
**Product:** UPI Lite  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A PSP callback latency increase on BHIM propagated into UPI Lite, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.42% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.98%.

## Contributing factors
- BHIM degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-PSP-LATENCY-018).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-14 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 01 Jun; validated in
RPT-D148-DAILY.

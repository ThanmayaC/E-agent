# RCA — RCA-2026-04 UPI Credit fraud / risk spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-04  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-04  ·  **Author:** Kavya Nanda
**Product:** UPI Credit  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A fraud / risk spike on iMobile Pay propagated into UPI Credit, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.32% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.71%.

## Contributing factors
- iMobile Pay degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-RISK-030).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-04 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 28 Sep; validated in
RPT-D99-DAILY.

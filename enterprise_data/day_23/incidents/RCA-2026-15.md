**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-15  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-15  ·  **Author:** Sunil Pillai
**Product:** UPI International  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A fraud / risk spike on FamApp propagated into UPI International, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.51% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.09%.

## Contributing factors
- FamApp degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-RISK-030).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-15 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 28 Jan; validated in
RPT-D24-DAILY.

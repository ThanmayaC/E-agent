# RCA — RCA-2026-24 UPI Credit merchant decline-rate spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-24  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-24  ·  **Author:** Kavya Nanda
**Product:** UPI Credit  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A merchant decline-rate spike on TravelGo, InsureWell propagated into UPI Credit, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.32% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.41%.

## Contributing factors
- TravelGo, InsureWell degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-MERCHANT-025).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-24 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 01 Nov; validated in
RPT-D133-DAILY.

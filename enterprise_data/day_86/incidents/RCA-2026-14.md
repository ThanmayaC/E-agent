# RCA — RCA-2026-14 UPI Autopay PSP callback latency increase

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-14  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-14  ·  **Author:** Rekha Joshi
**Product:** UPI Autopay  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A PSP callback latency increase on Axis Mobile propagated into UPI Autopay, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.43% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.02%.

## Contributing factors
- Axis Mobile degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-PSP-LATENCY-018).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-14 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 18 Sep; validated in
RPT-D89-DAILY.

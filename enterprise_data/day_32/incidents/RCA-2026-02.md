# RCA — RCA-2026-02 UPI Autopay fraud / risk spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-02  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-02  ·  **Author:** Rekha Joshi
**Product:** UPI Autopay  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A fraud / risk spike on HDFC MobileBanking propagated into UPI Autopay, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.49% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.61%.

## Contributing factors
- HDFC MobileBanking degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-RISK-030).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-02 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 08 Feb; validated in
RPT-D35-DAILY.

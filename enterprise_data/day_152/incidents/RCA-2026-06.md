# RCA — RCA-2026-06 UPI International bank timeout / 5xx spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-06  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-06  ·  **Author:** Sunil Pillai
**Product:** UPI International  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A bank timeout / 5xx spike on Punjab National Bank propagated into UPI International, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.63% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.21%.

## Contributing factors
- Punjab National Bank degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-BANK-FAILOVER-014).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-06 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 06 Jun; validated in
RPT-D153-DAILY.

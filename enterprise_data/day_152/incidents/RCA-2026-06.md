# RCA — RCA-2026-06 UPI Credit fraud / risk spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-06  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-06  ·  **Author:** Kavya Nanda
**Product:** UPI Credit  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A fraud / risk spike on HDFC MobileBanking propagated into UPI Credit, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.25% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.92%.

## Contributing factors
- HDFC MobileBanking degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-RISK-030).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-06 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 23 Nov; validated in
RPT-D155-DAILY.

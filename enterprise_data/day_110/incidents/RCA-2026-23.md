# RCA — RCA-2026-23 UPI Core merchant decline-rate spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-23  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-23  ·  **Author:** Deepak Ghosh
**Product:** UPI Core  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A merchant decline-rate spike on PowerPay Utilities, MetroRide propagated into UPI Core, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.37% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.55%.

## Contributing factors
- PowerPay Utilities, MetroRide degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-MERCHANT-025).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-23 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 26 Apr; validated in
RPT-D112-DAILY.

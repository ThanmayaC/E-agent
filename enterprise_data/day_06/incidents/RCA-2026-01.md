# RCA — RCA-2026-01 UPI Core bank timeout / 5xx spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-01  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-01  ·  **Author:** Deepak Ghosh
**Product:** UPI Core  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A bank timeout / 5xx spike on Punjab National Bank propagated into UPI Core, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 1.11% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.72%.

## Contributing factors
- Punjab National Bank degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-BANK-FAILOVER-014).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-01 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 12 Jan; validated in
RPT-D08-DAILY.

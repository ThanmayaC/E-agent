# RCA — RCA-2026-19 UPI Lite fraud / risk spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-19  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-19  ·  **Author:** Yash Gupta
**Product:** UPI Lite  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A fraud / risk spike on WhatsApp Pay propagated into UPI Lite, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.29% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.91%.

## Contributing factors
- WhatsApp Pay degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-RISK-030).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-19 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 16 Oct; validated in
RPT-D117-DAILY.

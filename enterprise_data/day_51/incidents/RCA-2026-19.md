# RCA — RCA-2026-19 UPI International fraud / risk spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-19  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-19  ·  **Author:** Sunil Pillai
**Product:** UPI International  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A fraud / risk spike on Google Pay propagated into UPI International, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.46% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.36%.

## Contributing factors
- Google Pay degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-RISK-030).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-19 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 25 Feb; validated in
RPT-D52-DAILY.

# RCA — RCA-2026-17 Merchant Products merchant settlement delay

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-17  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-17  ·  **Author:** Sunil Pillai
**Product:** Merchant Products  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A merchant settlement delay on IndusInd Bank propagated into Merchant Products, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.26% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.79%.

## Contributing factors
- IndusInd Bank degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-SETTLEMENT-022).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-17 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 16 Nov; validated in
RPT-D148-DAILY.

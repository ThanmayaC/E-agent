# RCA — RCA-2026-08 UPI Core fraud / risk spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-08  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-08  ·  **Author:** Deepak Ghosh
**Product:** UPI Core  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A fraud / risk spike on WhatsApp Pay propagated into UPI Core, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.49% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.87%.

## Contributing factors
- WhatsApp Pay degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-RISK-030).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-08 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 11 Feb; validated in
RPT-D38-DAILY.

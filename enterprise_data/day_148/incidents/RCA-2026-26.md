# RCA — RCA-2026-26 UPI Autopay fraud / risk spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-26  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-26  ·  **Author:** Rekha Joshi
**Product:** UPI Autopay  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A fraud / risk spike on FamApp propagated into UPI Autopay, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.26% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.57%.

## Contributing factors
- FamApp degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-RISK-030).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-26 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 17 Nov; validated in
RPT-D149-DAILY.

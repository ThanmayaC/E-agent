# RCA — RCA-2026-17 QR Payments PSP callback latency increase

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-17  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-17  ·  **Author:** Rekha Rao
**Product:** QR Payments  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A PSP callback latency increase on YONO SBI propagated into QR Payments, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.55% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.4%.

## Contributing factors
- YONO SBI degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-PSP-LATENCY-018).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-17 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 23 Mar; validated in
RPT-D78-DAILY.

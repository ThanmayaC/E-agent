# RCA — RCA-2026-25 UPI Lite X PSP callback latency increase

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-25  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-25  ·  **Author:** Deepak Patel
**Product:** UPI Lite X  ·  **Severity:** Sev-3

## Root cause
A PSP callback latency increase on BHIM propagated into UPI Lite X, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.35% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 99.65%.

## Contributing factors
- BHIM degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-PSP-LATENCY-018).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-25 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 11 Nov; validated in
RPT-D143-DAILY.

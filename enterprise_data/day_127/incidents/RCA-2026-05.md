# RCA — RCA-2026-05 UPI Credit bank timeout / 5xx spike

**RCA ID:** RCA-2026-05  ·  **Incident:** INC-2026-05  ·  **Author:** Kavya Nanda
**Product:** UPI Credit  ·  **Severity:** Sev-2

## Root cause
A bank timeout / 5xx spike on Union Bank of India propagated into UPI Credit, driving Technical
Declines to a peak of 0.58% on day 1 and
Success Rate to a low of 98.22%.

## Contributing factors
- Union Bank of India degraded during peak hours.
- Retry/failover thresholds were not tuned for this failure mode.

## Corrective actions
- Immediate: mitigation applied (documented in SOP-UPI-BANK-FAILOVER-014).
- Permanent: DEC-2026-05 — tune failover/retry and add alerting.

## Recovery
KPIs return to trend by 29 Oct; validated in
RPT-D130-DAILY.
